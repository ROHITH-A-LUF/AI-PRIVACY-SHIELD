How to Connect Your Real Android Phone

Enable USB Debugging to scan all downloaded apps on your device

Step 1

Connect USB Cable

Connect your Android phone to this computer using a USB cable. On your phone, set the USB notification mode to File Transfer (MTP).

Step 2

Enable Developer Options

On your phone, go to Settings > About Phone. Find Build Number (or MIUI / OS Version) and tap it repeatedly 7 times until you see "You are now a developer!".

Step 3

Turn On USB Debugging

Go to Settings > System / Additional Settings > Developer Options and toggle USB Debugging to ON.

Step 4

Authorize Your Computer

When the popup appears on your phone screen asking "Allow USB debugging?", check the box "Always allow from this computer" and tap Allow / OK.
